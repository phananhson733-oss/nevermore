<h2>Experiment Overview</h2>
<p>This is the first weekly experiment report in our build-in-public series.</p>
<p><strong>Hypothesis:</strong> Social channels (Reddit, X, LinkedIn) can validate content topic resonance within 7 days, providing signal for which topics to invest in for long-form SEO content.</p>
<p><strong>Duration:</strong> 7 days (March 3-9, 2026)</p>
<p><strong>Primary metric:</strong> Engagement rate per platform</p>

<h2>Setup</h2>
<p>3 content topics tested across 3 platforms:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Topic A:</strong> "The 80/20 of Growth Automation" (pillar: growth_automation)</li>
<li><strong>Topic B:</strong> "Why Most A/B Tests Are Statistically Invalid" (pillar: experiment_driven)</li>
<li><strong>Topic C:</strong> "Attribution Is Broken" (pillar: attribution)</li>
</ol>

<h2>Results by Platform</h2>
<h3>Reddit</h3>
<p>Topic A: 3,200 impressions, 7.1% engagement, 85 clicks. Topic B: 2,100 impressions, 4.3% engagement. Topic C: 1,800 impressions, 3.1% engagement. Baseline: 1.5%.</p>
<p>All three exceeded baseline. Topic A significantly outperformed.</p>

<h3>X (Twitter)</h3>
<p>Topic A: 4,800 impressions, 2.1% engagement. Topic B: 6,200 impressions, 3.4% engagement, 62 clicks. Topic C: 3,100 impressions, 1.8%. Baseline: 2.0%.</p>
<p>Topic B performed strongest on X -- contrarian, educational content resonated.</p>

<h3>LinkedIn</h3>
<p>Topic A: 1,400 impressions, 4.2%. Topic B: 1,100 impressions, 3.8%. Topic C: 980 impressions, 5.1%, 24 clicks. Baseline: 3.0%.</p>
<p>Topic C found its audience on LinkedIn -- marketing professionals who deal with attribution daily.</p>

<h2>Cross-Platform Attribution</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Reddit visitors:</strong> 3:42 avg time on page, 38% bounce, 2.8% email signup</li>
<li><strong>X visitors:</strong> 1:54 avg time, 62% bounce, 0.9% signup</li>
<li><strong>LinkedIn visitors:</strong> 4:15 avg time, 31% bounce, 3.4% signup</li>
</ul>
<p>LinkedIn delivered highest quality visitors despite lowest volume.</p>

<h2>Decisions</h2>
<ol>
<li>Double down on Reddit for growth automation content.</li>
<li>Use X for contrarian/educational content only.</li>
<li>Shift attribution content to LinkedIn.</li>
<li>Invest in long-form content for Topic A first.</li>
</ol>
