<h2>What Attribution Really Means</h2>
<p>Marketing attribution is the process of assigning credit for a conversion to the marketing touchpoints that influenced it. When a user signs up for your product, which of the 7 interactions they had with your brand over the past 30 days actually mattered? The answer depends on which attribution model you use.</p>
<p>Attribution matters because it determines where you spend money. If your last-click model says paid search drives 80% of conversions, you will invest heavily in paid search. But if those users first discovered you through a blog post, then saw a social thread, then clicked a retargeting ad -- the real driver was organic content.</p>

<h2>The Six Major Attribution Models</h2>

<h3>1. Last-Click Attribution</h3>
<p>100% of credit goes to the last touchpoint before conversion.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Simple funnels with 1-2 touchpoints.</p>

<h3>2. First-Click Attribution</h3>
<p>100% of credit goes to the first touchpoint.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Teams focused on top-of-funnel growth.</p>

<h3>3. Linear Attribution</h3>
<p>Equal credit distributed across all touchpoints.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Teams with limited data who want a balanced view.</p>

<h3>4. Time-Decay Attribution</h3>
<p>More credit goes to touchpoints closer to the conversion event.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> B2B products with long sales cycles (30-90 days).</p>

<h3>5. Position-Based (U-Shaped) Attribution</h3>
<p>40% credit to first, 40% to last, 20% distributed among middle touchpoints.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Most B2B SaaS products.</p>

<h3>6. Data-Driven Attribution</h3>
<p>Uses machine learning to calculate actual contribution of each touchpoint.</p>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> Products with high traffic and conversion volume.</p>

<h2>Choosing the Right Model for Your Stage</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pre-PMF (0-1,000 users):</strong> Use first-click attribution.</li>
<li><strong>Early growth (1,000-10,000 users):</strong> Switch to position-based (U-shaped).</li>
<li><strong>Scale (10,000+ users):</strong> Move to data-driven attribution.</li>
</ul>
