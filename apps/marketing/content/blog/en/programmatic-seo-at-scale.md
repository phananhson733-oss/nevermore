<h2>What Programmatic SEO Is (and Is Not)</h2>
<p>Programmatic SEO is the practice of generating large volumes of search-optimized pages using templates, structured data, and automation. Think about how Yelp has a page for every restaurant or Zillow for every listing. These are template-driven pages populated with structured data.</p>
<p>What programmatic SEO is not: spam. Each page must answer a genuine search query with useful information.</p>

<h2>Three Strategies for Programmatic SEO</h2>
<h3>Strategy 1: Glossary and Knowledge Base</h3>
<p>Create a page only for terms that deserve a distinct answer. A glossary-style library can use this approach, but each entry still needs original examples, useful context, and a clear reason to exist.</p>
<p><strong>Scale potential:</strong> 50-500 pages.</p>

<h3>Strategy 2: Comparison and Alternative Pages</h3>
<p>Create pages comparing your product with competitors. Target high-intent queries like "tool A vs tool B."</p>
<p><strong>Scale potential:</strong> 20-200 pages.</p>

<h3>Strategy 3: Location or Segment Pages</h3>
<p>Create dedicated pages for each market, industry, or region you serve.</p>
<p><strong>Scale potential:</strong> 50-5,000+ pages.</p>

<h2>Technical Implementation</h2>
<h3>1. Data Layer</h3>
<p>Structured data for every page: primary entity, unique content fields, metadata, SEO fields.</p>
<h3>2. Template Engine</h3>
<p>Each template needs: unique H1, structured content sections with genuine unique text, internal links, Schema.org data.</p>
<h3>3. Crawl and Index Management</h3>
<p>XML sitemaps, internal linking (3 clicks from homepage), proper pagination, robots.txt verification.</p>
<h3>4. Quality Assurance Pipeline</h3>
<p>Word count minimums, duplicate detection, broken link checks, schema validation, mobile rendering.</p>

<h2>Common Pitfalls</h2>
<p><strong>Thin content at scale.</strong> Invest in entity-specific content quality.</p>
<p><strong>Cannibalization.</strong> Use keyword mapping to ensure distinct targets per page.</p>
<p><strong>Orphan pages.</strong> Link every page from at least one category page.</p>
