<h2>The Starting Point</h2>
<p>In January 2026, astrologywiki.com was a blank canvas. The domain had been registered for two years but had zero indexed pages, zero backlinks, and zero organic traffic. The founder had deep domain expertise but no growth team, no content pipeline, and no marketing budget beyond the GenGrowth subscription.</p>
<p>The goal: reach 5,000 monthly active users within 90 days, entirely through organic channels.</p>

<h2>Phase 1: Discovery (Weeks 1-2)</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Keyword universe:</strong> 4,200 astrology-related keywords, 12.8 million combined monthly search volume.</li>
<li><strong>Content gap:</strong> Top 5 competitors averaged 340 indexed pages. astrologywiki.com had zero.</li>
<li><strong>Social signals:</strong> Reddit r/astrology had 1.2 million members. X averaged 45,000 astrology posts/day.</li>
</ul>
<p>The research produced 186 candidate opportunities. The team reviewed their evidence, relevance, and implementation constraints, then selected 30 for the first planning cycle.</p>

<h2>Phase 2: Strategy (Week 3)</h2>
<h3>Content Cluster Strategy</h3>
<ol>
<li><strong>Birth Chart Interpretation</strong> (1 pillar + 12 supporting articles)</li>
<li><strong>Planetary Transits</strong> (1 pillar + 8 supporting articles)</li>
<li><strong>Zodiac Compatibility</strong> (1 pillar + 24 supporting articles)</li>
</ol>
<p>Total planned: 49 pages across three clusters.</p>

<h2>Phase 3: Execution (Weeks 4-12)</h2>
<h3>Content Production</h3>
<p>Total published: 67 pages. Average word count: 1,800. Every article reviewed by the founder.</p>
<h3>Social Distribution</h3>
<ul>
<li>Reddit: 34 posts, 6.2% avg engagement (vs 1.5% baseline)</li>
<li>X: 48 threads, 2,800 avg impressions</li>
<li>Total social visits: 8,400 in first 8 weeks</li>
</ul>
<h3>Link Building</h3>
<p>12 earned backlinks from 45 prospects (27% conversion). Domain Rating: 0 to 18.</p>

<h2>Results: 14 Weeks Later</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>5,247 monthly active users</strong> (target: 5,000)</li>
<li><strong>67 indexed pages, 847 ranking keywords</strong> (23 in top-10)</li>
<li><strong>12,400 monthly organic sessions</strong></li>
<li><strong>Domain Rating 18</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Traffic breakdown: Organic 62%, Social 24%, Direct 11%, Referral 3%.</p>

<h2>Key Learnings</h2>
<ol>
<li>Content clusters beat isolated pages.</li>
<li>Social and SEO are complementary, not competing.</li>
<li>Long-tail keywords are the entry point.</li>
<li>Human review is non-negotiable for niche content.</li>
</ol>
