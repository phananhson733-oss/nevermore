# apps/marketing/src/components/task-center

Client components for the task center. The pages under `app/[locale]/tasks`, `promotions`, `earnings` and `admin/tasks` render on the server and use these only where the browser has to act.

- `clip-plaza.tsx` — the 素材广场 grid on `/tasks`: 9:16 cards with the clip's still (or the drama's cover), type and 本周爆款 badges (a clip named only by a UUID or hash shows no title); a card opens a wide two-column dialog. Left, in MoboReels' order: the drama's cover with its title, its chips (language first), 上架时间 · 共 N 集 · 前 M 集免费 and the full synopsis beside it (the synopsis under both on a phone), the two promo links from the claim panel, 系列资源 (numbered downloads of all the drama's clips, the one playing marked), the how-to as an open box, and a link to the drama's page. Right (first on a phone): the clip playing with its own download button. A signed-out visitor is asked to sign in. Links made in a dialog stay shown when it is reopened.
- `claim-panel.tsx` — a drama's two promo links as titled blocks, the way RS Boost pairs its App推广链接 and 资源推广链接: 推广链接 (`/t/<code>`, the drama's page on our site) and 资源推广链接 (`/t/<code>/go`, straight to the drama on its platform); no 口令. For a signed-in visitor without them, the panel POSTs to `/api/tasks/<id>/claim` as soon as it shows (once, with no button; the claim is idempotent), with a note in the boxes while that runs and a retry button when it fails; focus moves to the first copy button only after a retry. A 401 turns it into the signed-out state, which shows a sign-in link. Below, a link to the page the 推广链接 opens, so a promoter sees what readers get. It is never claimed during rendering, so a prefetch or crawler never gets a code.
- `link-block.tsx` — one promo link as RS Boost lays it out: title, where a click lands, the URL in a selectable box and a copy button (before the link exists, a dashed box with the note it is given: being made, failed, or shown once signed in), and a heading level (h3 straight under a page's or dialog's h2, h4 under an h3); takes the locale, not the copy object, because server pages render it.
- `drama-info.tsx` — stateless pieces of a drama's details shared by the dialog, `/tasks/<id>` and `/watch/<id>`: language, 频道, genre and tag chips, the 上架时间 · 共 N 集 · 前 M 集免费 line, numbered clip downloads (machine-named clips are labelled by number and size only) and the how-to.
- `task-tabs.tsx` — the 任务中心 / 我的推广 / 我的收益 tabs at the top of all three promoter pages (server component).
- `accounts-panel.tsx` — the 账号报白 section of /promotions: the promoter's TikTok/Facebook accounts with their status, the submit form, and withdraw for any still waiting for review.
- `admin/` — the admin backend's forms and controls; Chinese only.
